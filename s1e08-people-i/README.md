# Dreamer's Voyage Workshop, episode 7: people I

`workshop-episode-7.json` holds the show's people from this episode: Dreamer, Old Marla the lion tamer, Mayor Pettigrew and
Lucius Gilt. Each has basic info, hidden info, personality, a voice, a starting relationship and a place. Lucius has no location
on purpose: he stays off stage until the story brings him in. The portraits aren't in the file: an empty portrait gets drawn in
play, or upload your own. The server stores "known" for what Voyage's docs call "initialKnown"; both are accepted.

To try it: open a test world in Voyage Studio, go to Characters > NPCs > World NPCs, switch to the JSON view and paste the people
in. Their places (Mudbury, The Show's Camp, The Mayor's Garden) come from episode 5's map, so add that first. Save Draft. Use a
test world, not one people play.
