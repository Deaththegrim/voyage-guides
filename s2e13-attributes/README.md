# Dreamer's Voyage Workshop, episode 13: attributes

`workshop-episode-13.json` is the Workshop world's Attribute settings: Voyage's six attributes starting at 8 (16 at most at character
creation), constitution adding 2 health a point, strength 2% damage a point, dexterity 1% less damage taken a point, and Low Strength
and Low Charisma weaknesses at 2 or below.

To try it: open a test world in Voyage Studio, go to Mechanics > Attributes, switch to the JSON view and paste it in. Save Draft. Use
a test world, not one people play.
