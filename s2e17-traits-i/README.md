# Dreamer's Voyage Workshop, episode 17: traits I

`workshop-episode-17.json` holds the two creator pages from this episode, Background then Act (one pick each, Act in two sections), and their seven traits. The traits name the skills from episode 15, stamina from episode 16 and the moves from episode 14.

To try it: open a test world in Voyage Studio, go to Mechanics > Traits, switch to the JSON view and paste it in. Save Draft. Use a test world, not one people play.
