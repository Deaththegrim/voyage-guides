# Dreamer's Voyage Workshop, episode 30: publishing and updates

`workshop-episode-30.json` holds the Workshop's card: its card description, long description and tags.

To use it: open your world in Voyage Studio, click Publish up top, and copy each field into its box: `description` goes in Card description, `advancedDescription` in Description, and each of `tags` into Tags. Then write your own: it's your world's pitch.
